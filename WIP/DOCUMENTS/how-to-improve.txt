1. Normalise disease vectors
    - After summing, divide by the number of symptoms (i.e., use the average instead of the sum). This prevents longer symptom lists from dominating.
    - Also normalise each disease vector to unit length before storing (or during similarity calculation). This can sometimes help, but if directions are already similar, it won't change much.

3. Sum of vectors with small vocabulary (just 28 unique symptoms)

      If several diseases share the same (or very similar) sets of symptoms, their summed vectors will point in nearly the same direction.
      With only 8 dimensions, the space is too small to push them apart.
